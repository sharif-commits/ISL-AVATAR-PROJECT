Project Overview

This platform demonstrates real-time translation of everyday English sentences into Indian Sign Language using a 3D signing avatar. Each sentence below is mapped to its own sign sequence, so selecting different sentences produces a distinct, natural-looking performance rather than a generic animation.

The avatar reproduces full-body, hand, and expression detail for every sentence, so the sign performed carries the same meaning as the sentence you selected. Try a few different sentences below to see how the signing changes with each one.

Quick Guidelines

  • 1 Click and drag to select any of the dashed-underlined sentences below.
  • 2 Click the floating ⚡ Translate button that appears above your selection.
  • 3 The avatar widget will pop up in the corner and play the sign automatically.
  • 4 Use the play/pause button and slider on the widget to scrub through the sign.
